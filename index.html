How to count Exparel

The Exparel dose counts as a share of its own 266 mg maximum. For example, 133 mg uses 50% of the budget.

The share of the Exparel dose you treat as if it were the same dose of plain bupivacaine. Plain bupivacaine is not absorbed instantly either; its maximum dose already allows for normal absorption from the injection site. 100% treats Exparel exactly like plain bupivacaine, the most conservative choice. Lower values reflect your judgment that liposomal release adds less to peak blood levels.
